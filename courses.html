Frequently Asked Queries

What is the minimum eligibility criteria for DGCA certificate?

Applicants should meet the current DGCA age, education, and medical requirements for the selected remote pilot category.

How long is the DGCA Remote Pilot Certificate valid?

The certificate remains valid according to current DGCA regulations and the conditions attached to your operating category.

Do we need to bring our own drone equipment?

No. Training equipment and aircraft are provided at the academy for practical flight sessions.

What is the maximum student batch size per instructor?

Small practical batches keep instructor attention focused and provide meaningful hands-on flight time.
